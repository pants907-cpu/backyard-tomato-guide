Welcome to the garden

A healthy harvest starts with a few good habits.

Tomatoes can thrive in backyards, raised beds, and containers. You do not need a huge garden. You need a sunny spot, well-draining soil, steady care, and a plan that fits your space.

SeasonalTip

This week in the garden

Water the soil, not the leaves.

Give plants a deep drink near the base in the morning. Keeping leaves dry can reduce disease, while deep watering encourages strong roots.
