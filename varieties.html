Tomato Varieties

Choose a tomato that fits your table.

Some are made for snacking, some for sauce, and some for one perfect summer sandwich.

Start with growth habit

Determinate or indeterminate?

Compact and concentrated

Determinate

These plants grow to a limited height and produce much of their crop over a shorter period. They work well in containers and for batch harvesting.

Tall and continuous

Indeterminate

These vines continue growing and producing until frost or severe weather stops them. They need taller support and offer a longer harvest.

Make your choice

Match the plant to your priorities.

Space

For a patio or small bed, choose compact determinate or dwarf varieties.

Flavor

Try several colors and types. Sweet cherry and complex heirloom tomatoes taste very different.

Use

Grow paste tomatoes for sauce, slicers for sandwiches, and cherry tomatoes for snacks.

Timing

Check days to maturity. An early variety helps when the growing season is short.
