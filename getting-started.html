Getting Started

Give your tomatoes a strong beginning.

A good location and healthy soil solve many problems before they have a chance to start.

Choose the right place

Follow the sun.

Tomato plants generally need at least six to eight hours of direct sunlight each day. Morning sun helps dry dew from the leaves. Avoid low areas where water collects and locations that will become shaded.

Quick location check

  • Six to eight hours of direct sun
  • Easy access to water
  • Good airflow between plants
  • Soil that drains after heavy rain
  • Room for a cage or trellis

Pick your growing space

One plant, three good homes.

01

In the ground

Best with sunny yard space and soil that drains well. Mix compost into the soil before planting.

02

Raised bed

Provides control over soil quality and drainage, warms quickly, and is easier to reach.

03

Container

Use a large pot with drainage holes. Five gallons is a minimum for compact plants, and larger is better.

Keep it simple

Your basic supply list.

  • Healthy tomato seedlings
  • Compost-rich, well-draining soil
  • Trowel or small shovel
  • Tomato cage, stake, or trellis
  • Mulch such as clean straw
  • Watering can or gentle hose

Planting day

From seedling to garden in six steps.

  1. 01

    Harden off seedlings

    Slowly give indoor-grown plants more time outside for about a week.

  2. 02

    Water before planting

    Moist soil protects roots when you remove the seedling from its pot.

  3. 03

    Dig a deep hole

    Remove the lowest leaves and bury part of the stem to encourage more roots.

  4. 04

    Space plants properly

    Leave enough room for airflow and the plant's mature growth habit.

  5. 05

    Add support

    Install a cage, stake, or trellis while the plant is still small.

  6. 06

    Water and mulch

    Soak the root area and mulch without covering the stem.
